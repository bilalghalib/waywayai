## What This Platform Enables

### 1. **Self-Discovery Through AI Mirror**
You're not just training an AI - you're creating a mirror that shows you:
- "I always add pressure when I'm confident"
- "My 'burst mode' happens when drawing texture"
- "I have three distinct curve types"

**Current gap**: Data is collected but never reflected back in meaningful ways.

**What you need**:
- Stroke pattern visualization
- Style signature analysis
- Temporal evolution view ("you're getting bolder over time")

---

### 2. **Creative Multiplication**
Once the AI learns your style, you can:
- Draw 10 portraits in the time it took to draw 1
- Explore variations ("what if I drew this with more energy?")
- Test ideas without commitment ("would this composition work?")

**Current gap**: No generation capability exists.

**What you need**:
- Model that can generate drawings from references
- Imagination mode for concept exploration
- Style transfer for experimentation

---

### 3. **Process as Conversation**
Voice annotations transform drawing from silent practice to dialogue:
- "This is the tricky part" → AI learns where you struggle
- "Spike, spike, spike" → AI understands your rhythm
- "Actually, let me try again" → AI learns from your corrections

**Current gap**: No voice capture.

**What you need**:
- Real-time voice recording synced with strokes
- Speech-to-text for searchable transcripts
- Multimodal training that learns from your words + actions

---

### 4. **Building a Creative Asset**
You're creating something rare and valuable:
- A trained model that captures YOUR artistic voice
- A dataset of your creative decisions
- A tool that can amplify your creative output

**Current gap**: Training infrastructure doesn't exist.

**What you need**:
- Robust training pipeline
- Model versioning (track how it evolves)
- Generation API to actually use the trained model

## Refined Platform Requirements

Based on these sources of meaning, here's what matters MOST:

### Critical Features (Must Have):
1. **Real-time feedback** - You need to SEE what the AI learned
2. **Voice integration** - Essential for teaching context
3. **Pattern visualization** - Show you your own style signature
4. **Generation capability** - Use the trained model or it's pointless

### Important Features (Should Have):
5. **Two-panel interface** - Better workflow = more training data
6. **Training dashboard** - Track AI progress over time
7. **Email notifications** - Know when training completes

### Nice to Have (Could Have):
8. **Style transfer** - Apply your style to other images
9. **Collaborative drawing** - AI suggests next strokes
10. **Community features** - Share or compare models
